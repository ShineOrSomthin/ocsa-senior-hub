Universal Tips
1
Lead with your strongest workFirst and last impressions are everything — put your best piece there.
2
Your OCSA training is your edgeProfessional-level training since middle school. Name your conservatory — own it.
3
Research each school's cultureCalArts, USC Thornton, Berklee, and NYU Tisch each have very different vibes.
4
Record and get honest notesFilm practice auditions. Ask your conservatory director for a real critique.
Essay Writing Tips
Your artistic voice is your greatest asset — use it
1
Write like an artist, not a studentSound like you — not a template. Authenticity beats vocabulary every time.
2
Start in the middle of a momentA rehearsal, a performance, backstage. Skip the thesis opener.
3
Small story, big meaningOne specific afternoon can carry a huge theme. Don't list everything you've done.
4
Show growth, not perfectionHow you responded to rejection is more compelling than a highlight reel.
5
Get 3 readers before submittingConservatory director, a classmate, and someone outside your art world.
Get Feedback on Your Draft
Share your Google Doc link and a human reviewer will leave comments directly on your doc.
BEFORE YOU SEND
In Google Docs, click Share → change access to "Anyone with the link" → set role to Commenter. Otherwise the reviewer won't be able to open or comment on your doc.
SAT / ACT Help
Many arts colleges are test-optional — here's what you need to know
Test-Optional at Top Arts Schools
All UC campuses are test-free (since 2021). All CSU campuses are test-free (since 2022). CalArts, Berklee, NYU Tisch, and most private arts colleges are test-optional. Always confirm each school's current policy before applying.

Coping Tips for Arts Students
Use your art as an outlet
Create something just for you — imperfect, unfinished, not for any audition or grade.
Protect non-application time
At least one hour a day that belongs only to you — not apps, not rehearsal, not homework.
Sleep is not optional
Chronic sleep deprivation tanks auditions, essays, and judgment. It's not a badge of honor.
Rejection is not failure
Every working artist has been rejected. A college rejection is not a verdict on your talent.

Privacy Policy
Orange County School of the Arts — OCSA Senior Hub  ·  Effective May 25, 2026
Summary — what matters mostWe collect only what's needed to run this app. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. Student data is used only for school purposes. Users under 13 must have parental consent. You can delete your data at any time by contacting us.
1. Who We Are
The OCSA Senior Hub ("the App") is operated by or on behalf of Orange County School of the Arts (OCSA), located at 1010 N. Main St, Santa Ana, CA 92701. The App is a student resource tool created for OCSA seniors. Questions about this policy should be directed to OCSA Student Services at studentservices@ocsarts.net.
2. Information We Collect
When you create an account, we collect: name, email address, conservatory, encrypted password, and optional college goal. We also store your checklist progress and mood check-ins (private, never shared with staff).
We do not collect: Social Security numbers, government IDs, financial information, biometric data, location data, device identifiers, or browsing history.
3. How We Use Your Information
We use your data only to operate and personalize the app, save your checklist progress across sessions, and allow OCSA staff to respond to support questions you submit.
We never use your data for advertising, marketing, or commercial purposes. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for their own use.
4. Children Under 13 COPPA
This App is directed at high school seniors (generally ages 17–18). We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), enforced by the Federal Trade Commission (FTC). Users under 13 may not create an account without verified parental or guardian consent. If we become aware that a child under 13 has created an account without parental consent, we will promptly delete that account and all associated data. Parents may contact studentservices@ocsarts.net to request review and deletion.
5. Student Records FERPA
This App does not access, store, or process official OCSA education records (grades, transcripts, disciplinary records, or enrollment status). Information you voluntarily enter is self-reported and not maintained by OCSA as part of your official FERPA-protected student record. When students turn 18, FERPA rights transfer directly to the student.
6. Data Storage & Security
Your account data is stored using browser-based storage on the platform hosting this App. Passwords are encrypted before storage. Mood check-ins are stored only in your private account and are never visible to OCSA staff, other students, or administrators.
7. Your Rights California / CCPA
Under California law (including the California Consumer Privacy Act / CCPA), you have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal information at any time. Update your profile in the app, or contact studentservices@ocsarts.net for deletion requests. We will respond within 30 days. We do not sell data, so there is nothing to opt out of in that regard.
8. Cookies & Tracking
This App does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics services. We use browser storage (not cookies) to maintain your login session and save your checklist progress. We do not track you across other websites.
9. Third-Party Services
Google Calendar — if you choose to add an event to Google Calendar, you are redirected to Google's own service governed by Google's privacy policy. Google Fonts — fonts (DM Sans, Patrick Hand) are loaded from Google's font service; your IP may be received by Google as part of font delivery. No personal account data is shared. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising networks.
10. Changes to This Policy
If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and notify users through the app. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
